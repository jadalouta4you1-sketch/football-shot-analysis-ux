<!--@slide 01   MATCH QUESTION-->
# Football Shot Analysis for Training Decisions

**Which shooting zone deserves video review?**

A university coach has one match and a short preparation window. Choose one pattern, explain the evidence and save it for discussion. Football makes the cost of a misleading percentage visible.

---

<!--@slide 02   ILLUSTRATIVE FIXTURE-->
# Two goals or the higher rate?

**Inside: 2 / 10 = 20%      Outside: 1 / 2 = 50%**

Inside produced more goals. Outside has the higher conversion rate, but just two attempts. These illustrative numbers support choosing clips to investigate. They cannot establish a training prescription.

---

<!--@slide 03   SIMULATED DISCOVERY-->
# Three roles exposed different uncertainties

**Counts  /  marker meaning  /  save status**

In an AI-generated discovery exercise, P1 looked first at the percentage, P2 needed the marker key and P3 sought confirmation. The working persona has enough football knowledge to read a pitch, but limited time to check every denominator.

---

<!--@slide 04   COMPETITOR OBSERVATION-->
# The archive makes the fixture hard to identify

**34 links share the label “Premier League”**

A direct inspection of the England results archive found repeated league names and no level 1–3 headings. Six keyboard tabs still preceded the first league download. Our match picker therefore includes opponent and date.

---

<!--@slide 05   DESIGN DECISION-->
# One route takes a candidate to video review

**Match → compare zones → review → saved**

Pair counts with rates. Keep a table-only view for comparison and a nearby legend for the combined view. Zero shots, tied rates and unavailable data each need a different explanation.

---

<!--@slide 06   AI-GENERATED TEST-->
# The first exercise fell short in both layouts

**Table  2/3             Table + map  2/3**

The target requires the correct higher-rate zone and its goals and attempts within 60 seconds. In the simulation P3 omitted the denominator in the table. P2 took 67 simulated seconds in the combined view.

---

<!--@slide 07   ITERATION-->
# Make the selection and its outcome explicit

**A named zone before save. A named receipt after.**

The earlier designer walkthrough found that saving gave no visible confirmation. The revised companion repeats the match and zone on success. It also explains marker meaning and displays both counts alongside each rate.

---

<!--@slide 08   SIMULATED RETEST-->
# The scripted retest cleared the threshold

**3/3 correct within 60 simulated seconds**

P1: 46 s. P2: 52 s. P3: 57 s. With three roles, meeting the 80% target requires all three. The timings were authored for the exercise. Whether the map speeds up a coach remains open.
