# Discovery, persona and journey

The design question was whether to lead with conversion rate, shot counts or both. The discovery exercise uses three scripted roles. Yousef (P1), Hemam (P2) and Ahmed Al-Jarmoozi (P3) are the names used in the scenarios. Their dialogue and actions are illustrative.

**Interview guide.** Ask a coach or student analyst about the last match they reviewed. How did they choose a clip? Which numbers did they check, where did they find them, and what happened after they chose a pattern? For a live interview, explain the purpose, ask permission to take notes and keep only an anonymous participant code.

| Cluster | Role / simulated statement | Scenario observation | Interpretation → design consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence for a recommendation | P1 “I picked the biggest percentage first.” | Selected the 50% row before reading two attempts | Small denominators can mislead → put counts and rates together |
| Reading space | P2 “Which dots went in?” | Could locate the penalty area but hesitated over markers | Location and outcome are separate questions → add a solid/open legend and a table alternative |
| Closing a task | P3 “Where can I see what I saved?” | Looked back at the summary to verify the zone | A generic confirmation leaves the choice uncertain → repeat the match and zone on success |

**Working persona derived from the exercise.** A university coach preparing a debrief recognizes pitch zones and scoring rates but has little time to reconstruct a denominator. The coach needs one defensible clip-review candidate and risks turning two shots into a tactical recommendation. The role's knowledge and time pressure remain assumptions. The archive audit adds a need to identify the match and season before comparing numbers.

| Journey step | Likely feeling | Friction | Design opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find the match | Hurried | Similar fixtures | Opponent and date |
| Compare two zones | Curious | Rate hides volume | Goals and attempts in one row |
| Interpret the pitch | Uncertain | Markers have two meanings | Legend next to map |
| Save one candidate | Cautious | Review and saved states look similar | Separate action and named receipt |
