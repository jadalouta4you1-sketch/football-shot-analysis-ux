# Problem and success criterion

A university football coach is choosing clips before a team debrief. Against Northside, two of ten shots inside the box went in (20%). Outside the box, one of two went in (50%). The higher percentage is worth looking at, but two shots are too few to justify a change in training.

| Question | Project decision |
|---|---|
| Who needs this? | A coach preparing a short video discussion. |
| What does the coach need? | Choose a zone and explain the goals and attempts behind its rate. |
| What does the staff need? | Start the debrief with a specific sequence to review. |
| What gets in the way? | A percentage hides how few shots produced it. |
| How is success scored? | At least 80% correctly identify the higher-rate zone and give its goal and attempt counts within 60 seconds, without help. |

**Assumptions.** The counts come from an illustrative fixture. The short preparation window is a design assumption. The biggest uncertainty is whether seeing a pitch map helps the coach explain the result faster. That is why the prototype offers the same numbers with and without the map.
