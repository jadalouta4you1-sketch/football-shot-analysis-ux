# Three simulated reviews of one match task

**Method.** Yousef (P1), Hemam (P2) and Ahmed Al-Jarmoozi (P3) name the three roles in this scripted walkthrough. The dialogue, decisions and timings are illustrative. The designer’s earlier walkthrough is documented separately.

## Discovery exercise

Decision: should the first comparison emphasize conversion, counts, or both? Prompt each role with “Walk me through your last match review. How did you choose a clip, and what did you check before sharing it?” The three hypothetical answers model different breakdowns.

| Cluster | Role / simulated statement | Scenario observation | Interpretation → design consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence for a recommendation | P1 “I picked the biggest percentage first.” | Selected the 50% row before reading two attempts | Small denominators can mislead → put counts and rates together |
| Reading space | P2 “Which dots went in?” | Could locate the penalty area but hesitated over markers | Location and outcome are separate questions → add a solid/open legend and a table alternative |
| Closing a task | P3 “Where can I see what I saved?” | Looked back at the summary to verify the zone | A generic confirmation leaves the choice uncertain → repeat the match and zone on success |

**Working persona derived from the exercise.** A university coach preparing a debrief recognizes pitch zones and scoring rates but has little time to reconstruct a denominator. The coach needs one defensible clip-review candidate and risks turning two shots into a tactical recommendation. The role's knowledge and time pressure remain assumptions. The archive audit adds a need to identify the match and season before comparing numbers.

| Journey step | Likely feeling | Friction | Design opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find the match | Hurried | Similar fixtures | Opponent and date |
| Compare two zones | Curious | Rate hides volume | Goals and attempts in one row |
| Interpret the pitch | Uncertain | Markers have two meanings | Legend next to map |
| Save one candidate | Cautious | Review and saved states look similar | Separate action and named receipt |

## Label check and first informal trial (simulated)

P1 interpreted “Conversion” as goals scored until shown “2 goals / 10 shots.” P2 understood “Select pattern” as selecting a dot. P3 thought “Save candidate” would save the whole match. Use “Which zone will you review on video?”, “Review selection” and “Save for video review.” In the first trial the surprising issue was that a correct zone choice could still have a weak explanation. Scoring the explanation catches this.

## Moderated comparison and retest

Scenario: tomorrow's training starts with a video discussion. Choose a zone to investigate, explain which zone had the higher rate and how many goals and attempts support it, then leave your selection ready for the video session. Allow either zone as the candidate if justified. Prompt once at the end for how the reviewer knows it was saved. Brief for any live replication: “We are reviewing the interface. Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time. With your permission I will keep anonymous task notes.”

Score the comparison from task start to explanation: correct higher-rate zone AND correct numerator/denominator AND within 60 seconds unassisted. Score save comprehension separately. Target ≥80%; with three roles that requires 3/3. A = Northside (outside 1/2 vs inside 2/10). B = Riverside (inside 1/3 vs outside 2/9). Each row below is **scripted**, including all timings.

| Role | First layout / fixture / response | Second layout / fixture / response | Save observation in initial scenario |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Table / A / outside, 1 of 2, 48 s ✓ | Both / B / inside, 1 of 3, 50 s ✓ | Identified completion |
| P2 | Both / A / outside, 1 of 2, 67 s × | Table / B / inside, 1 of 3, 53 s ✓ | Identified completion |
| P3 | Table / B / inside, omitted attempts, 44 s × | Both / A / outside, 1 of 2, 55 s ✓ | Mistook review for completion |

Both layouts score **2/3** in this scenario. Neither reaches 80%. Task order is only partially balanced with three roles. Simulated times do not estimate time savings.

**Retest scenario after clarifying the legend, counts and saved state** (both layout): P1 B, inside 1 of 3, 46 s; P2 A, outside 1 of 2, 52 s; P3 B, inside 1 of 3, 57 s. Each role identifies the exact saved zone on the confirmation screen. The scripted retest meets 3/3 in this exercise. Repeat exposure and authored outcomes prevent a causal claim. A future live test should use fresh fixtures and counterbalanced order.
